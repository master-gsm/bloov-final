TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

IndexedDB Stores:
  - operationQueue      (INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE tracking)
  - dataCache          (Local record storage)
  - transactionLog     (Audit trail)
  - conflictLog        (Conflict records)
  - syncState          (Metadata)
  - financialState     (Pending transactions)

Sync Strategy:
  - Online detection   (navigator.onLine + health checks)
  - Auto-sync interval (30 seconds)
  - Retry mechanism    (3 attempts, 1s delay)
  - Conflict resolution (offline-first policy)

Connection Quality:
  - excellent: < 100ms latency
  - good:      < 300ms latency
  - poor:      < 1000ms latency
  - offline:   no connection

Financial Rules:
  - Commissions: pending → calculated → synced
  - Cash: pending_movement → recorded → synced
  - Period locking: prevents operations during close

Immutable Tables (no direct delete):
  ✅ sales, purchases, expenses
  ✅ cash_registers, cash_transactions
  ✅ partner_settlements, employee_commissions
  ✅ operating_expenses, setup_expenses
